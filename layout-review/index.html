1 · Reading the building

Before any wall moves, four facts about this house decide what is possible.

  • Party wall west, windows north, east and south. The 0.22 m west wall is the half of a double-leaf party wall. Every daylit room must sit on the east or south face; the south face looks onto the garden. The south-east corner of each floor is the best room in the house, and on the DG it is currently the smallest one.
  • Stair. The partitions that move are the 0.10–0.12 m ones. Both answers change nothing in Option A, O1 or E1; they decide the French door and whether you may ever remove the OG partition.
  2. Plumbing. Option A, O1 and E1 keep every wet room on the west party wall stack. Extend the stack 2.5 m south for O1 and add a second riser for the washer-dryer column. Only B or C need a new DG riser through the OG north bedroom's walk-in.
  3. Order of works. OG bath first (it is the only rule failure that needs a plumber), then the DG partitions, then the EG strip. The EG kitchen and living side need no work.
  4. Bed wall systems. Specify the DG headboard panel with integrated night tables, lighting, switches and sockets at 0.3 m depth; the hotel sources treat this as the one element guests notice, and it is the feng shui buffer on the bath side.
  5. Doors. Pocket doors need a 1.0 m cavity beside the opening; every pocket door drawn has it. Bath doors opening outward (EG guest bath, OG WC room) take a free-occupied indicator lock.

What this review could not verify

  • Dimensions come from screenshots at ±5 cm. Have the partition positions set out from the as-built survey, not from these numbers.
  • The stair direction was inferred from the arrival-landing logic (a flight cannot arrive flush against the DG north wall). If a flight runs the other way, the only change is that the OG sofa slides 1 m.
  • Roof slopes, parapets and the walkability of the set-back strips are not visible in the plans.
  • The research agents could only read search extracts, not full pages; every figure above is tied to the URL that showed it, and VDI 6000-1:2024-07, DIN 18040-2 and Neufert should be checked in the primary text before they go into a tender.
